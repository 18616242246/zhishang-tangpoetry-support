隐私政策

「纸上唐诗」是一款完全离线运行的唐诗三百首阅读、赏析与背诵应用，支持简体中文、繁體中文与 English 界面。我们高度重视您的隐私：

  • 不收集任何个人数据：本应用无需注册账号，不包含广告、统计分析或第三方追踪 SDK，也不会将您的任何阅读、收藏、摘句或背诵记录上传至任何服务器。
  • 纯本地存储：您的收藏、书签、圈点摘句、温习计划与阅读偏好仅保存在您设备本地数据库中，卸载应用后将随之清除。
  • 相册权限说明：仅当您主动使用「诗笺成图」功能保存图片到相册或从相册自选背景图时，系统会请求相应的相册写入权限或调用系统照片选择器；图片处理完全在设备本地完成，不上传网络。
  • 应用内购买：本应用的一次性买断内购完全由 Apple App Store（StoreKit 2）安全处理与本地验证，我们不会获取或存储您的任何支付或 Apple ID 信息。

Tang Poetry · Privacy Policy & Support

Version: 1.1.0 · Updated: November 2026

Privacy Policy

Tang Poetry (纸上唐诗) is a completely offline reading, commentary, and memorization companion for 300 Tang Poems, available in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and English. We respect your privacy:

  • No Personal Data Collected: The app requires no account, contains no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs, and never transmits your reading history, favorites, excerpts, or study schedules to any server.
  • On-Device Storage Only: Bookmarks, favorites, saved excerpts, reading preferences, and spaced-review plans are stored exclusively in a local database on your device and deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Photo Library Access: Requested solely on-device when you explicitly save a Poetry Card image to Photos or select a custom background image via the system photo picker. All image rendering happens offline on your device.
  • In-App Purchase: The optional one-time “Paper Atelier” unlock is processed and verified exclusively by Apple App Store via StoreKit 2. We never access or store your payment details or Apple ID.
