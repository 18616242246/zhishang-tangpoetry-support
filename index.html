隐私政策（Privacy Policy）

「纸上唐诗」是一款完全离线运行的唐诗三百首阅读、赏析与背诵应用。我们高度重视您的隐私：

  • 不收集任何个人数据：本应用无需注册账号，不包含广告、统计分析或第三方追踪 SDK，也不会将您的任何阅读、收藏、摘句或背诵记录上传至任何服务器。
  • 纯本地存储：您的收藏、书签、圈点摘句、温习计划与阅读偏好仅保存在您设备本地数据库中，卸载应用后将随之清除。
  • 相册权限说明：仅当您主动使用「诗笺成图」功能保存图片到相册或从相册自选背景图时，系统会请求相应的相册写入权限或调用系统照片选择器；图片处理完全在设备本地完成，不上传网络。
  • 应用内购买：本应用的一次性买断内购完全由 Apple App Store（StoreKit 2）安全处理与本地验证，我们不会获取或存储您的任何支付或 Apple ID 信息。

English Summary (Privacy & Support)

Tang Poetry on Paper (纸上唐诗) is a completely offline Chinese classical poetry reading and study app designed for Chinese-language readers worldwide.

  • No Data Collection: The app requires no account, contains no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs, and never transmits any personal or usage data to any server.
  • Local Storage Only: Bookmarks, favorites, highlights, reading preferences, and review schedules are stored exclusively on your device and removed if you uninstall the app.
  • Photo Library Access: Used solely on-device when you explicitly choose to save a generated poetry card image to Photos or select a background photo from your library via the system photo picker.
  • In-App Purchase: The optional one-time unlock is processed and verified exclusively by Apple App Store via StoreKit 2.
